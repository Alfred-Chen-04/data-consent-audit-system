## Recording Rule

- Keep `review_status` as `pending` until an actual reviewer response exists.
- Do not copy `recommended_default` into `confirmed_decision` without explicit
  reviewer confirmation.
- Record non-listed answers with the `other` option and explain them in `notes`.
- Do not change poster claims solely because the sheet exists; revise the poster
  only after confirmed decisions are recorded.

## What Confirmed Answers Unlock

- Framing and evidence-card answers determine the final poster structure.
- The contrast-case answer determines whether CNN, Booking.com, and NerdWallet
  remain labeled contrast cases, are treated as unresolved, or are excluded.
- The unresolved-review answer determines whether the current limitations stay
  visible or must be resolved before stronger claims.
- The final-print answer determines the next revision pass.

Until those fields are completed, the existing pilot/method framing and claim
guardrails remain the safe default.
