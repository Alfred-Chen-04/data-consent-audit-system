## Final Research Conclusion

RQ1 produced a traceable four-component framework for path availability, path
effort, transparency, and choice effectiveness. RQ2 found dated,
component-level evolution, not a single industry-wide trajectory. Five of six
selected historical cases improved at least one component and one later
regressed; all three first-layer cases reduced refusal asymmetry. The evidence
does not support a universal, continuous, or causally uniform movement toward
stricter or looser consent systems.
