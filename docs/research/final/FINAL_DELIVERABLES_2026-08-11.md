## Final Result

RQ1 produces a traceable four-component ruler for availability, effort,
transparency, and choice effectiveness. RQ2 applies the ruler to dated states
while grading direction separately from explanation.

The local five-site pilot validates the workflow but remains directionally
insufficient. The separate six-company case series finds five component-level
improvements and one functional regression. These selected cases support
continuous component-level auditing, not a web-wide prevalence or universal
causal claim.
