## Final Claim Boundary

Safe final statement:

> The controlled pilot validates a traceable capture-and-comparison method but
> remains directionally insufficient. In a separate purposive six-company case
> series, five cases improve at least one audited component and one functionally
> regresses. Concrete interactions that regulators can specify and verify show
> the clearest documented improvement, while consent quality remains partial
> and reversible.

Do not convert this statement into an internet-wide rate, randomized causal
effect, or legal compliance judgment.
