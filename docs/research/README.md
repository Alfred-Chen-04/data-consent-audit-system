## Interpretation Rule

The five-site local matched pilot and the six-company retrospective case series
are separate evidence lanes. The local pilot validates the method but all five
direction labels remain `insufficient_evidence`. The six selected primary-source
cases support five component-level improvements and one functional regression;
they do not establish a population prevalence rate or universal cause.

Do not use an earlier plan, email draft, paper candidate, or poster mockup as a
final finding when a corresponding file exists under `final/`,
`presentation/`, or `poster/`.
