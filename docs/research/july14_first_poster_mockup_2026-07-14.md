## Poster Scope

The left column explains why consent interfaces matter, the capture-to-version
pipeline, and the deterministic-scoring guardrail. The middle column gives the
current evidence snapshot and shows Guardian and Coca-Cola as banner-present
method evidence cards. The right column labels CNN, Booking.com, and
NerdWallet as no-visible-first-screen-banner contrast cases, then places the
limitations and next decisions beside them.

The poster correctly says that the evidence package is pilot/method evidence,
not a final 20-site dataset. It does not claim legal compliance, a complete
final dataset, synced raw HTML, a current live-site state, or banner-path
failure for any no-visible-banner contrast case.
